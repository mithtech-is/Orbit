# MVP Scope and Roadmap

## MVP Scope

The MVP includes tenant setup, RBAC, team management, lead/outlet/territory management, route planning, mobile route execution, consent-based tracking, live manager map, geofenced visits, visit evidence, Medusa-backed catalogue/order visibility, offline sync, notifications, reports, audit logs, privacy controls, demo data and automated tests.

## Out of MVP

AI route recommendations, AI lead allocation, advanced territory balancing, CRM/ERP connectors, field payment collection, gamification, payroll integration, multi-language support, sophisticated GPS spoofing detection and advanced reporting studio are later phases.

## Phase Plan

| Phase | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 0 | Research alignment, architecture contract, diagrams and implementation plan. |
| 1 | Monorepo foundation, local infrastructure, shared packages, seed-data foundation and baseline validation. |
| 2 | Multi-tenant authentication, RBAC, tenant middleware and audit foundation. |
| 3 | Leads, outlets, territories, map provider and manager views. |
| 4 | Route planning, assignments, visits, check-in/check-out and monitoring. |
| 5 | Consent, work sessions, mobile background tracking, ingestion and live map. |
| 6 | Product catalogue, inventory and Medusa field-order workflows. |
| 7 | Offline sync engine, conflicts, idempotency and attachment recovery. |
| 8 | Reports, Electron operations, notifications, retention, observability and hardening. |
